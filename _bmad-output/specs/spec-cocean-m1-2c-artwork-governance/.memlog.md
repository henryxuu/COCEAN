# M1.2c Artwork Governance Memlog

## 2026-08-14 — Scope lock

- M1.2c only governs the effective artwork shown and delivered by COCEAN.
- NAS media files, embedded artwork, sidecars, directory names, and Music manifests remain read-only.
- Candidate sources are current local observations, administrator uploads, and Cover Art Archive artwork for an already confirmed MusicBrainz Release.
- Apple Music, arbitrary image URLs, AI-generated artwork, bulk unattended replacement, and source-file write-back are excluded.
- Artwork uses an independent `artworkRevision`; it must not share identity `revision` or field `metadataRevision`.
- User selection and explicit hide survive rescans and primary-version changes. Automatic fallback follows current observed versions.
- New delivery jobs freeze the effective artwork bytes. Existing queued/running jobs never re-read artwork governance.
- Real FNOS schema 19 remains an explicit authorization gate.
